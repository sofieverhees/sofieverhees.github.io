Welcome to my website!

I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of British Columbia, supervised by <a href='https://www.math.ubc.ca/profiles/anotida-madzvamuse'>Anotida Madzvamuse</a>. Previously I obtained my PhD at the Maxwell Institute Graduate School (<a href='https://www.mac-migs.ac.uk/'>MAC-MIGS</a>) in Edinburgh. Here, I worked on the coupling between chemical and mechanical signalling in cells using reaction-diffusion equations and the equations of linear elasticity under the supervision of <a href='https://www.macs.hw.ac.uk/~mp91/'>Mariya Ptashnyk</a> and <a href='https://www.maths.sussex.ac.uk/~cv42/'>Chandrasekhar Venkataraman</a>.
